Privacy Policy

Customised Technologies (P) Ltd · Last updated: 29 September 2026

Customised Technologies (P) Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), makers of the Rapid-I precision measuring systems, operates a WhatsApp customer-care service. We use it to follow up with customers after a service visit and confirm that they received their invoice and calibration certificate. This policy explains what information we handle and how.

2. Information we collect

  • Your name and business contact details (such as your WhatsApp phone number), taken from our existing service and sales records.
  • Service details linked to your account, such as whether a service visit or calibration was completed and whether the related documents were sent.
  • Your replies to our WhatsApp messages, for example whether you received your invoice and calibration certificate.
  • Basic delivery information provided by WhatsApp, such as whether a message was delivered or read.

We do not ask for payment card details, passwords, government ID numbers or other sensitive personal information through WhatsApp.

3. How we use your information

  • To send you customer-care messages about your service, invoice and calibration certificate.
  • To record your confirmation in our service records and resolve any missing documents.
  • To improve our after-sales service and meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers needed to run this service:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), to send and receive messages. Meta handles message data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Microsoft (OneDrive), where our service records are stored.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the server that processes messages.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed for the purposes above and for our business, tax and warranty records, then delete or anonymise it.

6. Security

We restrict access to our records and server to authorised staff and protect credentials and access tokens. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your choices and rights

  • To stop receiving messages, reply STOP on WhatsApp or email us. We will stop contacting you through this service.
  • You may ask to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

8. Data deletion request

To have your information removed from this WhatsApp service, email rapidi@customisedtechnologies.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Include your name, company name and the WhatsApp number you want removed. We will confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days. We may keep limited records where the law requires us to, such as invoices and tax records.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

For any privacy question, write to rapidi@customisedtechnologies.com or call +91-80-2852-2858.
